About Aqeeq Decor

Elegant interiors, tailored furnishings, and décor that feels personal.

Aqeeq Decor helps transform residential and commercial spaces through thoughtfully selected curtains, wallpapers, furniture, blinds, mattresses and flooring solutions. We blend aesthetic direction with practical execution so every space feels refined, functional and complete.

Who We Are

Designing spaces that balance comfort, detail and identity.

Aqeeq Decor is focused on helping clients create warm, stylish and functional interiors through curated décor categories. Whether you are furnishing a new flat, upgrading a single room or completing a larger commercial environment, we guide the entire process with attention to quality, budget and usability.

We believe the right décor should not only look elegant, but also fit the way a space is actually lived in. Our role is to simplify selection, offer practical recommendations and deliver outcomes that feel complete and cohesive.

  • Personalized product guidance
  • Solutions for residential & commercial spaces
  • Balanced approach to budget and quality
  • Coordinated décor across multiple categories
Service Categories

Décor categories that shape a complete interior experience.

Each category is selected to help clients create a space that feels layered, coordinated and visually refined — from soft furnishings to surface finishes and furniture elements.

Curtains

Custom soft-furnishing solutions that frame the room with warmth, privacy and elegance.

Wallpapers

Statement wall finishes that add texture, tone and personality to both modern and classic interiors.

Furniture

Bespoke and practical furniture solutions for stylish homes, offices and hospitality spaces.

Window Blinds

Functional and elegant blind systems for light control, privacy and a cleaner visual finish.

Mattress

Comfort-driven options designed to support restful living while complementing bedroom styling.

Floorings

Durable and visually polished flooring selections to anchor the character of every room.

Why Choose Us

A décor partner that brings together style, clarity and execution.

We focus on making décor decisions easier for our clients by recommending the right categories, finishes and combinations for each type of space. Our strength lies in understanding how design should look, feel and function in real life.

From first conversation to final installation, we aim to provide a polished and supportive experience that helps clients feel confident in every choice.

Our Approach

How we help clients shape their space with confidence.

Our process is built around understanding the client, recommending the right décor mix and ensuring the final look feels thoughtful and complete.

Understand the Space

We begin with the layout, the purpose of the room and the client’s preferred aesthetic direction.

Recommend the Right Fit

We suggest categories, materials and styling options that work beautifully within the project goals.

Complete the Experience

We support the final execution so the finished space feels polished, layered and well resolved.
