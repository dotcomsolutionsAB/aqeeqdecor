About Aqeeq Decor

About Us
Who We Are

Designing elegant spaces with comfort, function & detail

Aqeeq Decor is dedicated to transforming residential and commercial spaces with thoughtfully selected furnishing and décor solutions. From curtains and wallpapers to bespoke furniture, flooring and blinds, we help clients build spaces that feel refined, warm and practical.

Our approach combines design guidance, material knowledge and on-ground execution. We focus on helping every client choose the right products for their space, style and budget without compromising on visual appeal or long-term usability.

Why Choose Us

A trusted décor partner for homes and commercial spaces

We work closely with clients to understand the space, category requirements and styling direction before recommending products. Our objective is to make décor selection easier, more practical and more aligned with each client’s expectations.

Whether you are furnishing a new flat, upgrading a room, or finishing a larger project, Aqeeq Decor focuses on balancing design, budget, quality and timely execution.
