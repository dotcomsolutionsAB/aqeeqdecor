About Aqeeq Decor

Designing elegant spaces that feel refined, comfortable and truly personal.

Aqeeq Decor transforms residential and commercial interiors through thoughtfully selected curtains, wallpapers, furniture, window blinds, mattresses and flooring solutions. We bring together style, practicality and execution to create spaces that feel complete and timeless.

OUR STORY

We help clients turn ideas into harmonious spaces by carefully combining materials, finishes and furnishing elements that reflect both lifestyle and visual identity.

Spaces with character

Aqeeq Decor focuses on creating interiors that feel warm, elegant and thoughtfully resolved. Every category we offer is selected to work not just individually, but as part of a complete décor story.

From selecting the right drapery style to recommending surface finishes, furniture tones and flooring harmony, our approach is both aesthetic and practical.

Design with intention

We believe every room should feel like it belongs to the people who live or work in it. That is why our recommendations are always shaped around the client’s style, layout, practical use and comfort.

Our goal is simple — to make décor selection easier, more refined and more rewarding from start to finish.

OUR SERVICES

Each category contributes to a balanced and elegant interior. Together, they help build spaces that feel complete, layered and visually cohesive.

Curtains

Custom drapery solutions that add softness, privacy and sophistication to every space.

Wallpapers

Statement wall finishes that bring texture, depth and personality to modern and classic interiors.

Furniture

Elegant furniture selections designed to balance comfort, function and visual harmony.

Window Blinds

Practical and polished blind systems that offer light control with a clean finished look.

Mattress

Comfort-driven selections crafted to support restful living and refined bedroom styling.

Floorings

Durable, elegant flooring options that anchor the overall tone and character of a room.
