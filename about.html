OUR STORY

At AQEEQ Decor, we believe every space tells a story of comfort, elegance, personality and timeless design. From our furnishing studio in Bhawani Peth, Pune, we help homes and commercial spaces feel luxurious, functional and uniquely personal.

2016

The Beginning

AQEEQ Decor began with a simple philosophy: beautiful interiors should feel personal, timeless and effortlessly elegant.

2018

Premium Furnishing

Our offering expanded across designer curtains, premium blinds, wallpapers, upholstery, sofa fabrics, flooring solutions and furnishing accessories.

2020

Custom Solutions

We refined our made-to-measure approach with personalized consultation, careful material selection and tailored recommendations for every space.

2022

Execution Excellence

Our process grew stronger with precise measurements, professional installation and polished finishing for residential and commercial interiors.

2024

Refined Living

We strengthened our luxury design language through curated fabrics, textures, blind systems, wallpaper designs and elegant furnishing details.

Present

Serving Pune

Today, we serve Bhawani Peth, Camp, Wanowrie, Kondhwa, NIBM, Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Hadapsar, Magarpatta and nearby Pune locations.
